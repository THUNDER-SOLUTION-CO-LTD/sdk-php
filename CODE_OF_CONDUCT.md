# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Everyone who takes part in issues, pull requests and discussions is expected to follow it.

In short: be respectful and patient, give and accept constructive feedback, and focus on what is best for the people who use this SDK. Harassment, insults, discrimination and publishing other people's private information are not accepted.

## Reporting

To report behaviour that breaks this code of conduct, contact the maintainers privately through the [Thunder contact page](https://developer.thunder.in.th/contact). Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits and issues that break this code of conduct, and may block people who break it repeatedly or seriously.
